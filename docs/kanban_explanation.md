# Kanban Board Explanation – ServiceHub AI

## What is a Kanban Board?

A Kanban board is a visual project management tool used to track tasks as they move through different stages of development. It uses columns such as "To Do", "In Progress", and "Done" to represent workflow stages and provide visibility into task progress.

---

## How the Board Visualizes Workflow

The ServiceHub AI Kanban board represents different stages of development through columns. Tasks move from the backlog to active development and eventually to completion. This provides a clear overview of project progress at any given time.

---

## Work-in-Progress (WIP) Limits

To prevent bottlenecks and improve efficiency, the board limits the number of tasks in the "In Progress" column to a maximum of three tasks. This ensures that work is completed before new tasks are started, improving focus and productivity.

---

## Support for Agile Principles

The Kanban board supports Agile principles by enabling continuous delivery, flexibility, and transparency. Tasks can be updated and reprioritized easily, allowing the system to adapt to changing requirements while maintaining steady progress.

The board also enhances collaboration by clearly showing responsibilities and task ownership, ensuring that all team members are aligned with project goals.
