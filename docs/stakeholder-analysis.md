# Stakeholder Analysis – ServiceHub AI

ServiceHub AI is a digital service marketplace designed to connect customers with local service providers. To ensure the system meets real-world needs, key stakeholders were identified and their concerns analyzed.

| **Stakeholder** | **Role** | **Key Concerns** | **Pain Points** | **Success Metrics** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customers | Individuals who search and book services such as repairs, tutoring, or cleaning. | Easy service discovery, secure payments, reliable providers. | Difficulty finding trustworthy providers; lack of centralized booking platforms. | Faster service discovery; successful bookings completed within minutes. |
| Service Providers | Professionals offering services through the platform. | Ability to reach more customers, manage bookings efficiently, and receive timely payments. | Limited exposure to potential clients; manual booking management. | Increased booking volume and simplified scheduling. |
| System Administrators | Manage the platform operations and ensure compliance with policies. | Monitoring system usage, verifying service providers, maintaining platform integrity. | Difficulty detecting fraudulent providers and managing user issues. | Efficient moderation and improved platform reliability. |
| Platform Owners | Organization responsible for maintaining and growing the platform. | Revenue generation, system scalability, and user growth. | Difficulty scaling operations without automated tools. | Increased active users and transaction volume. |
| IT Operations Team | Responsible for system deployment, monitoring, and maintenance. | System uptime, scalability, and performance. | Infrastructure challenges during high traffic periods. | System availability above 99.5% uptime. |
| Payment Gateway Provider | External service responsible for processing transactions. | Secure payment integration and reliable transaction processing. | Failed transactions due to poor system integration. | Secure and reliable payment processing with minimal transaction failures. |
| Customer Support Team | Handles user issues and platform complaints. | Ability to resolve disputes and assist users quickly. | Lack of centralized data when assisting customers. | Faster resolution of user queries and reduced complaint rates. |

The analysis above ensures that the design of ServiceHub AI addresses the needs of all major stakeholders interacting with the platform.
